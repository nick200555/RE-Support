1. System Overview
Project Summary
The Real Estate Support System (RSS) is a custom Frappe application built on top of ERPNext v15+.
It covers the complete post-handover real estate support value chain — from buyer complaint intake
through defect rectification, possession management, RERA escalation, and buyer self-service portal
— integrating natively with ERPNext's Accounts, CRM, Quality, HR, and Communication modules.

Module 1 — Complaint Management
Manages the complete support ticketing lifecycle for real estate buyers. Handles complaint intake via
the buyer portal, WhatsApp, or CRM. Auto-assigns to the correct team based on category, tracks SLA
breach, sends buyer notifications at every status change, and escalates critical tickets to the RERA
module. This is the primary operational module used daily by the support team.

Module 2 — Defect & Snagging
Manages the complete post-handover punch-list process. Site inspectors log defects per unit with
photographs, area classification, and severity rating. Defects are assigned to contractors with due
dates. A QC re-inspection confirms rectification. Buyer provides digital sign-off on the completed
report. All defect data feeds into the project-wise defect heatmap report.

Module 3 — Possession Management
Manages the end-to-end possession workflow from initial triggering through key handover. The
process spans Finance (dues clearance), Legal (NOC collection), Construction (OC availability), and
the Possession Executive (scheduling and key handover). Six workflow states enforce departmental
sign-offs before the next step can begin. Buyer receives WhatsApp notification at each milestone.

Module 4 — RERA Escalation
Tracks complaints formally filed with the Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA). Each RERA
Complaint links to the originating support ticket. The Legal team manages the response preparation,
hearing schedule, and outcome recording. Penalties and penalty payment tracking are integrated with
ERPNext Journal Entries. Dashboard flags high-risk complaints for management attention.
RERA Complaint
 Transaction
 DocType ·
Submittable ·
High Priority
Created when a support ticket escalates to formal RERA proceedings. All legal communications, hearing dates, and responses are
tracked here. Non-compliance can attract crore-scale penalties.

SLA Engine — Key Methods (utils/sla_engine.py)
# re_support/utils/sla_engine.py
import frappe
from frappe.utils import add_to_date, now_datetime
def set_sla_due_dates(doc, method=None):
    """Auto-set SLA response and resolution deadlines on ticket creation."""
    if not doc.sla_policy:
        cat = frappe.get_doc("Ticket Category", doc.category)
        doc.sla_policy = cat.default_sla
    if doc.sla_policy:
        sla = frappe.get_doc("SLA Policy", doc.sla_policy)
        now = now_datetime()
        doc.sla_response_due   = add_to_date(now, hours=sla.response_time_hrs)
        doc.sla_resolution_due = add_to_date(now, hours=sla.resolution_time_hrs)
def check_sla_breaches():
    """Scheduled job: flag breached tickets and escalate."""
    now = now_datetime()
    open_tickets = frappe.get_list("Support Ticket",
        filters={"status": ["in", ["Open","Assigned","In Progress"]]},
        fields=["name","sla_resolution_due","escalate_to"])
    for ticket in open_tickets:
        if ticket.sla_resolution_due and ticket.sla_resolution_due < now:
            frappe.db.set_value("Support Ticket", ticket.name,
                "resolution_breached", 1)
            if ticket.escalate_to:
                send_breach_alert(ticket.name, ticket.escalate_to)
    frappe.db.commit()
